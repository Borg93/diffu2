# MOSS-Image runs: the experiment log

One entry per run: the question it answers, exactly how its cache and training were set, its Slurm jobs and its
folder, then its state and results. The trainings ran on Leonardo, the drawing and speed tests on the DGX Spark (their
rows say so). Paths are relative to the project's `$WORK` on Leonardo, or under `~/diffu_queue` on the Spark; each run
folder also holds a `run.json` with this entry's facts, beside the trainer's own records (a cache's `cache.json` holds
its options, each checkpoint's `state/progress.json` the training settings). How to run them:
[MOSS_HPC.md](MOSS_HPC.md).

## What matters now (as of the entries of 20 Nov 2026)

- **The model** is the sample-above run (`moss/runs/full_above2mp`): a full fine-tune from the released Base at 2 MP,
  the sample page's tokens placed above the page's, 10,257 steps; every later test draws with its step 10,257 EMA. It
  rests on the audit's rebuilt 2 MP cache and the sibling pilot's `above` arm from Base.
- **What it draws**: the layout and the text as given, the paper and the kind of scan from the sample, the pen's weight
  from the sample; the letter forms follow the sample on some kinds of page and not on running text, and on the demo
  Space's new pages the hand does not follow and a faint sample's ink comes out too dark.
- **Guidance**: 2 rather than MOSS-Image's 3 (the ink closer to the sample's, the same words); nothing tried beat plain
  guidance 2, and guiding only the first half of the steps draws the same page in about a quarter less time.
- **Distillation**: 8-step unguided LoRA students. The Space keeps the first (a, step 116); d and e became their
  teachers by step 200 and are no closer in ink on the Space's pages; g and h (`--swap 0.5`) were queued on 20 Nov, f
  waits for room in the queue.
- **Speed**: the student's LoRA in bfloat16, the leaner RMSNorm, compiled blocks, MXFP8, UniPC and a step cache, each
  measured on the Spark ("Drawing speed").
- Earlier recipes (B and the other 1 MP runs, the hand tests on B, the pilot arms that lost, cancelled runs) are one
  line each under "Superseded runs" at the end.
