What worked, what did not, and what we learned

Two pools of archive scans, 3.15 million in all, were read years ago by the old YOLO and TrOCR pipeline. We want to turn them into pretraining data for a whole-page reader. This page gives every experiment so far its verdict: why we ran it, what came out, and what it taught us.

Where it stands, {{STATE}}. The cut into single pages is solved for about nine openings in ten. A first set of 139,937 pages with a trusted cut is pretraining Nemotron 3.5 Parse ({{PROGRESS}}). The score that says whether it helped comes on 18 November.
