Is the reader getting better?

Characters wrong on the same 200 hand-transcribed test pages; lower is better. Grey: the old pipeline. The last step, pretraining on pool pages with their old labels, gained 0.15 points, which is within chance. A blind review of 107 pages found our reader's text more faithful than the old label on 60 and worse on 5, so the next test trains on the reader's own readings instead.
